Formal Two-Person Anchoring Script
ANCHORS

Anchor 1 (A1): Good morning/afternoon/evening everyone!

Anchor 2 (A2): A very warm and pleasant welcome to one and all gathered here for this wonderful occasion — Freshers’ Fest 2K26!

A1: Today is not just another event. It is the beginning of a beautiful journey filled with new friendships, new experiences, unforgettable memories, and exciting opportunities.

A2: Exactly! A new chapter begins today for our first-year students, and we are delighted to have all of you here to make this day truly special.
🌸 WELCOMING THE GATHERING

A1: On behalf of our entire college family, we extend our heartfelt welcome to our respected Dean, HODs, esteemed faculty members, beloved seniors, and our wonderful juniors.
A2: Your presence adds immense value and happiness to this celebration. We are truly honoured to have you all with us today.

A1: And to our dear juniors — a very special welcome to you!

A2: You have officially become a part of our college family. We hope your journey here will be filled with learning, growth, success, friendship, and countless beautiful memories.

A1: And to our seniors, thank you for being here to welcome and encourage our juniors.

A2: Your guidance, support, and friendship will surely make their college journey even more meaningful.


(DEAN)

A1:May we now request our respected Dean, [Dr.  A.Sri krishna ], to kindly address the gathering and share a few words of wisdom with our students.
   Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome our respected Dean with a huge round of applause!
A2: Thank you, Sir/Madam, for your valuable and inspiring words.

(HOD)
A2:We are honoured to invite our respected Head of the Department, [Dr. sri A Nagesh garu], to address the gathering.

Sir/Madam, we request you to kindly share your thoughts and blessings with our freshers.
A1: Thank you, Sir/Madam, for your encouraging words and valuable guidance.

(lamp lighting)

Anchor 2:
“May we now have the honour of inviting our respected Principal Sir, Dr. [Name], to kindly come forward and inaugurate this ceremony by lighting the ceremonial lamp.”

Anchor 1:
“Sir, we humbly request you to grace the stage and light the lamp, symbolising the beginning of a bright and successful journey for our dear freshers.”
A2:Thank you sir.


(inviting principle sir)
“Ladies and gentlemen, it is our pleasure and privilege to warmly welcome our respected Principal Sir, whose gracious presence adds great honour to this occasion.

Sir, we extend our heartfelt welcome to you. We are truly delighted to have you with us today. Let us all welcome our respected Principal Sir with a huge round of applause.”
